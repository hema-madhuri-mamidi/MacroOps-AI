# MacroOps AI

## GenAI-Powered Fulfillment & Last-Mile Operations Intelligence Platform

MacroOps AI is a proposed operations intelligence platform designed to help fulfillment and last-mile operations teams detect operational exceptions, understand potential root causes, monitor SLA risk, access contextual SOP guidance, and receive data-grounded recommendations.

The project is being developed as part of the MacroEdtech GenAI Research Internship — Phase 3.

---

## Project Objective

MacroOps AI aims to transform fragmented operational data into actionable operational intelligence through the following intelligence loop:

**Detect → Understand → Predict → Recommend → Act**

The platform focuses on improving operational visibility, reducing manual investigation, supporting root-cause analysis, and enabling human-reviewed operational recommendations.

---

## Business Problem

Fulfillment and last-mile operations involve multiple interconnected stages such as:

- Order processing
- Inventory availability
- Picking
- Packing
- Rider assignment
- Delivery
- Exception handling

Operational information can become fragmented across these stages, making it difficult for operations teams to identify emerging SLA risks, investigate exceptions, understand potential causes, and locate relevant operational guidance quickly.

MacroOps AI is designed to provide a consolidated intelligence layer across these operational stages.

## Data

The project uses **synthetic operational data** for development and evaluation.

The core operational datasets represent areas such as:

- Orders
- Inventory
- Picking
- Packing
- Delivery / Rider Operations
- Exceptions
- Operational / SOP Knowledge

Supporting datasets include Stores and Order Items.

No real customer or production operational data is used in the current prototype.
